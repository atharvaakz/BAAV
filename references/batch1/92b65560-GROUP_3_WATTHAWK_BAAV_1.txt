01                               INDUSTRY OVERVIEW


 The global power industry was valued at $2.3 trillion USD in
 2023 and is expected to reach $3.7 trillion USD by 2030,
 growing at a CAGR of 4.6% (2023–2030).

 The Indian power industry, with a total installed capacity of
 ~472 GW as of March 2025, is projected to grow at 5 – 5.5%
 CAGR through 2030. India third-largest electricity producer
 globally and aims to achieve 500 GW of non-fossil fuel
 capacity by 2030.

 The Indian power industry as of mid‑2025 is approximately
 $245 billion USD in total market size shared by 48 listed
 Indian companies as of today.

     Across the three firms, Adani Power shows the strongest financial turnaround post-2022.
        Key ratios like Net Profit Margin, ROA, ROCE, and ROE, which were negative until 2021, have now
        crossed ideal benchmarks.
        Operating margin remained steady at 21%, and a zero dividend payout supports its reinvestment-
        heavy growth strategy.

     Torrent Power presents a mixed picture.
        NPM and OPM at 8% and 23% are slightly below ideal, but ROA, ROCE, and ROE have
        outperformed since 2016.
