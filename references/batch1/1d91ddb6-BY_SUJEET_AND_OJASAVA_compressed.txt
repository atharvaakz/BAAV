                           BLIND SPOT ANALYSIS
JSW ENERGY
 Heavy dependence on thermal assets may lead to stranded asset risk as India shifts faster toward clean energy.
 ESG pressure and stricter decarbonization policies can increase financing costs and reduce investor interest.
 Delayed coal exit may result in asset write-downs and weaker long-term valuation.
 Faster transition into renewables, storage, and green hydrogen is necessary to stay competitive.
 Strategic coal asset sales and a clear net-zero roadmap can reduce this risk.
NHPC
 Overdependence on Himalayan hydropower creates high climate and hydrological risk.
 Changing rainfall and glacier patterns may reduce actual generation below expected levels.
 Revenue predictability may weaken if old hydrology assumptions fail in future planning.
 Environmental clearances and inter-state water disputes can further delay projects.
 Faster diversification into solar and pumped storage hydro is critical for risk reduction.

SUZLON ENERGY
 Strong focus on onshore wind may limit growth if offshore wind expands rapidly.
 Lack of commercial offshore wind technology gives global players like Vestas an advantage.
 Delay in developing 5 MW+ turbines may reduce competitiveness in future tenders.
 Missing early offshore opportunities can permanently shrink Suzlon’s addressable market.
 Investment in offshore technology and strategic partnerships is urgently needed.

 CONSUMER ANALYSIS FRAMEWORKS
JSW ENERGY
 Major customers are State DISCOMs through long-term PPAs, where tariff competitiveness and payment security are key.
 Growing focus on C&I corporate buyers like steel, cement, and data centers seeking renewable power for ESG goals.
 Better margins are possible in C&I compared to traditional DISCOM contracts.
 Emerging demand from Green Hydrogen off-takers like fertilizer, petrochemical, and steel companies creates future growth.
 Target of 20 GW generation capacity by 2030 supports expansion across all customer segments.
NHPC
 Primary consumers are State Electricity Boards and DISCOMs, with regulated tariffs under CERC.
 Installed capacity of 7,700+ MW ensures stable long-term power supply to utilities.
 Growing focus on Grid Operators through Pumped Storage Hydro (PSH) for balancing solar and wind power.
 State governments in Himachal, Uttarakhand, and Arunachal are key partners for hydro project development.
 Strong demand from storage and balancing services increases NHPC’s future relevance.

SUZLON ENERGY
 Core customers are Independent Power Producers (IPPs) and renewable developers building utility-scale wind farms.
 Holds nearly 30% domestic wind market share with over 20,000 MW installed capacity.
 Strong demand from C&I buyers like cement, steel, and textile firms for captive wind power.
 PSUs like SECI and NTPC Renewable are major customers through government tenders.
 Its end-to-end model (manufacturing + O&M) improves repeat orders and customer retention.
                         VALUE CHAIN ANALYSIS
JSW ENERGY
 Starts with project development, land acquisition, and approvals for solar, wind, hydro, and thermal assets.
 Strong EPC execution helps control project cost and improve returns in competitive tariff-based bids.
 Operates diversified generation portfolio with a target of 20 GW capacity and 40 GWh storage by 2030.
 Power is sold through long-term PPAs and merchant markets, balancing stable and high-return revenues.
 Support activities include green hydrogen, battery storage R&D, finance management, and regulatory affairs.
NHPC
 Begins with hydrological surveys and river basin identification for hydro project development.
 Core strength lies in dam construction, tunnels, and powerhouses in difficult Himalayan terrain.
 Operates 7,700+ MW installed capacity with low variable cost and long asset life of 35+ years.
 Focus on power scheduling, grid balancing, and growing Pumped Storage Hydro (PSH) pipeline.
 Environmental management, specialized engineers, and SCADA-based monitoring are key support functions.

SUZLON ENERGY
 Starts with turbine R&D focused on larger and more efficient 3 MW+ wind turbines.
 Manufactures blades, nacelles, and towers domestically, reducing import dependence and improving margins.
 Strong EPC execution ensures smooth installation and commissioning of wind farms.
 O&M business manages 20,000+ MW installed capacity, creating high-margin recurring annuity income.
 Digital platform iSpin and strong vendor management improve predictive maintenance and customer retention.

WACC METHODOLOGY
Raw beta from 60-month regression of monthly stock returns
against Nifty 50 using the SLOPE function, then cross-verified via
the Covariance method (COV/VAR) for robustness.
Blume's adjustment applied to correct for mean-reversion bias —
raw beta weighted 67% and market beta (1.0) weighted 33%,
pulling extreme betas toward the market over time.
Unlevered beta strips capital structure risk using book D/E and
tax rate, isolating pure business/asset risk across all three firms
for a like-for-like comparison.
Re-levered at market value D/E (not book) to reflect current
investor-perceived capital structure — market weights are
forward-looking and more appropriate for CAPM.
Cost of debt estimated via synthetic rating — ICR (EBIT/Interest)
mapped to Damodaran's rating table to assign BBB/BB/A−
ratings, with default spreads India-adjusted by adding country risk
premium over US base.
Market value weights used for WACC (not book) — equity
weight = MCap/(MCap+Debt), ensuring the blended cost
reflects what the market actually prices in, not historical
accounting values.
